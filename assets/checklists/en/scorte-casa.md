title: Home supplies (2 weeks)
order: 2
---
To stay at home for two weeks without shops, running water or electricity. Use the "Supplies" tool to calculate quantities.

## Water
- 4 liters per person per day in sealed containers
- Unscented bleach (to disinfect water)
- Bathtub and pots to fill as soon as an alert is issued

## Long-life food
- Pasta, rice, dried legumes
- Canned food (legumes, tuna, meat, vegetables)
- Oil, salt, sugar, honey
- Biscuits, crackers, nuts and dried fruit
- UHT or powdered milk
- Baby food and pet food, if needed
- Camping stove with spare gas cartridges

## Health and hygiene
- Complete first aid kit
- Regular medicines for one month
- Painkillers and fever reducers
- Soap, toothpaste, sanitary pads, diapers
- Heavy-duty bags and cat litter or sawdust for an improvised toilet

## Power and light
- LED flashlights and lanterns with batteries
- Candles and matches (beware of fire risk)
- Power bank and, if possible, a foldable solar panel
- Battery-powered or hand-crank radio

## Home
- Duct tape and plastic sheeting (to seal a room)
- Fire extinguisher
- Adjustable wrench to shut off gas and water
- Blankets and sleeping bags
- Basic tools (hammer, screwdriver, pliers)
