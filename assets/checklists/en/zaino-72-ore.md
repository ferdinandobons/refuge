---
title: Emergency backpack (72 hours)
order: 1
---
One backpack per person, ready by the door, to survive 3 days if you have to leave home suddenly.

## Water and food
- Water: at least 2 liters per person, plus a water bottle
- Water purification tablets or portable filter
- Ready-to-eat food for 3 days (snack bars, nuts and dried fruit, pull-tab cans)
- Cutlery, metal cup

## Health
- First aid kit with gauze and bandages
- Tourniquet
- Personal medicines for at least 7 days and copies of prescriptions
- Emergency foil blanket
- FFP2 masks
- Oral rehydration salts
- Hand sanitizer, wet wipes, toilet paper

## Light and communications
- Headlamp with spare batteries
- Charged power bank and cables
- Battery-powered or hand-crank radio
- Whistle
- List of important phone numbers written on paper

## Documents and money
- ID card, passport, health insurance card
- Photocopies of documents in a waterproof bag
- Cash in small notes
- Paper map of the area with meeting points marked
- Spare house and car keys

## Clothing and shelter
- Full change of clothes, wool socks, underwear
- Waterproof jacket, hat, gloves
- Sturdy, broken-in shoes
- Large trash bags (shelter, rain cover, container)

## Tools
- Multi-tool pocket knife
- Lighter and windproof matches
- Duct tape
- Rope (10 m)
- Pen and notebook
