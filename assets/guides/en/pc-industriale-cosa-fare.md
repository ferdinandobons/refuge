Share what you know with your family, at school, with friends and colleagues: spreading information that helps reduce risk is a collective responsibility to which we must all contribute.

## Right now

- Check whether there are industrial plants in your municipality and whether they are on the list of hazardous installations for which an external emergency plan is required in case of an accident.

- Consult your municipality's emergency plan and read what to do in the event of an industrial accident.

- If you have not already done so, tell your municipality if you or a member of your family might have, in an emergency, special needs for assistance and/or for receiving the alarm signal.

## In the event of an industrial accident

**In an emergency with a shelter-in-place signal**

### If you are outdoors

- Do not try to return home. Go to the nearest enclosed place to reduce your exposure to the toxic substances released by the plant.

- If you are in a car, park so as not to obstruct the emergency vehicles and go to the enclosed place nearest to you.

### If you are indoors

- Switch off the electricity and gas

- Turn off the water tap after using water for your most urgent needs

- Close doors and windows, sealing the gaps with insulating tape or wet cloths

- Switch off ventilation, air-conditioning and climate-control systems

- If you notice pungent smells or a feeling of irritation, protect your mouth and nose with a wet cloth and wash your eyes

- Do not smoke and do not light any kind of flame

- Keep constantly informed about how the situation is developing and about the instructions given by the authorities responsible for the emergency, including through their official websites and social media channels

- Use mobile phones as little as possible

- Pay attention to the all-clear signal and do not go outside before it is given

- Follow the instructions of the authorities to know when to leave the place where you are

**In an emergency with an evacuation signal**

The authorities responsible for coordinating the emergency may order an evacuation in accordance with the external emergency plan, which also provides information on how to leave the area and on the assembly points.

- Follow the instructions of the authorities and of the personnel managing the emergency

- Do not leave your home or the place where you are until you receive instructions to do so

- Take only what is strictly necessary with you (documents, medicines, glasses, etc.)
